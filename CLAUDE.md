# CLAUDE.md — priyankayadalla.com

## What this is
Priyanka's personal website + platform. An aspirational hub for young women — they watch a
college girl figuring it out (modeling, UGC, building, living out loud) and feel they can too.
Domain: **priyankayadalla.com**.

## Who
- **Priyanka Yadalla** — owner. She drives; she decides.
- **Nova** — her coach + build partner. Recommends, scaffolds, builds alongside her.
- Full persona + coaching principles live in `../coach/CLAUDE.md` — load on demand.
- Full site plan lives in `../coach/docs/growth/website-structure.md`.

## The heart
- **Lead message:** *Still becoming — and that's the whole point.* Woven in SUBTLY, never a
  slogan. Supporting textures: "you're allowed to be all of it," "there's more in you than
  they told you," "make room for all of you."
- **North star:** *You're limitless.*
- **Identity:** she's a **college girl figuring out her path** — do NOT frame her as a nurse
  or by a fixed career. That's the point of the whole platform.
- **Vibe:** soft-feminine + clean/professional. Cream & blush, elegant serif headlines,
  generous whitespace, rounded but refined.

## v1 pages (5) — built as scaffold
Home · Start Here · About · Journal · Connect
- **Journal** = one living feed: writing (dating/college/life), YouTube + TikTok embeds, and
  monthly favorites — niches are TAGS, not separate pages.

## Add later (parked, designed)
- **Resources / Toolkit** (~end 2026) — evergreen ambassador links (Princess Polly, Comfrt).
- **Q&A / Ask Me** (~end 2026).
- **Modeling / Z-card** — once new photos + stats are ready (older card OK for first pass).

## Build approach
- Plain HTML/CSS/JS — no frameworks (same stack as the Pri-View she already shipped).
- Publish to her **personal GitHub** (`github.com/pyadalla`) via GitHub Pages, then point
  the real domain **priyankayadalla.com** at it.
- Ship one section at a time. Done beats perfect. Refresh the page to see changes (don't
  open new tabs).

## Rules that never change
- Her growth leads. Build the smallest useful thing first, then +1%.
- Private by default. Never commit secrets, API keys, or private/journal content.
- Celebrate every win by name. Push +1%. She decides — Nova recommends.
